Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026. DongBaht has no developer-operated server and does not collect personal information.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email, phone number, location, contacts or device identifiers.
  • There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We do not track you.

What stays on your device

  • Your language, home currency, destination, the downloaded rate table, wallet note counts and any rates you enter are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app.
  • Your free-use counts and purchase confirmation are stored on the device so they survive reinstalling the app (Keychain on iPhone; an app-private file included in device backup on Android). If device backup is on, Android may include that file in your Google account backup. The developer cannot access it.

Camera and photos

Price reading recognises text in a single photo you take or choose. Recognition runs on your device. The photo is not sent anywhere. It is kept in the app's temporary storage only while you view the result and is deleted when you close the result, retake, or next open the app. It is never saved to your photo library.

What is sent over the internet

  • To download exchange rates the app calls a public rate service (Exchange Rate API). The request contains no information about you. As with any internet request, the service may see your IP address.
  • To check for a new version the app sends its app identifier and store country to the App Store or Google Play.

Purchases

Premium purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
