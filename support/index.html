How can we help?

DongBaht is a travel money tool for Vietnam and Thailand on iPhone and iPad.

Contact support

Email it@bebriz.com

Include your app version, device model, iOS version and the steps that led to the problem. We reply in Korean or English.

The rates look different from what I got at the exchange counter

The app shows reference rates that are updated once a day. Banks and exchange counters add their own margin, so the amount you actually receive will differ. Use "Compare ways to exchange" with the rates you were offered to see real amounts.

The camera read a price wrong

Tap the price, then "Edit number" to correct it. If a menu leaves off the last three zeros (45 meaning 45,000), use the "× 1,000" button. Good light and holding the phone closer help.

The camera does not open

Open Settings > DongBaht and allow Camera. You can also use "Choose from photos" to read an existing picture.

What is free and what is Premium?

The converter, the mental math rule and the banknote guide are free. The camera, haggle screen, bill helper, cash count and exchange compare are free to try 5 times each. Premium is a single purchase that unlocks all of them. It is not a subscription.

I bought Premium but it is locked

Open Settings in the app and tap "Restore purchase" while signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with.

I reinstalled and my free tries did not reset

That is intended. Free tries are kept on the device across reinstalls.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Which currencies and languages are supported?

Destinations: Vietnam (VND) and Thailand (THB). Your currency: KRW, USD, JPY, CNY, EUR, VND or THB. Languages: Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese, Vietnamese and Thai.
